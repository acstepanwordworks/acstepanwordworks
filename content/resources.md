## Texas Czech Legacy Project

I volunteer with the Texas Czech Legacy Project, which uses AI-assisted transcription to preserve recordings of Texas Czech, an endangered Czech dialect spoken in rural Texas, continuing a family legacy of language preservation that traces back to my great-grandfather Jan Štěpán, who emigrated from Bohemia in 1889.

**[Visit the Texas Czech page →](/texas-czech/)** for the recording of our National Museum of Language talk, the slides, and my writing on the project.
